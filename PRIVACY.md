# CULMS Simple privacy policy

CULMS Simple is an unofficial browser extension for students of Chandigarh
University. It is not made by or affiliated with Chandigarh University.

## What it reads

The extension only runs on pages of `lms.cuchd.in`. On those pages it reads
what the LMS already shows you: your name, your enrolled courses, their units,
files and activities, your calendar events, and the status of your quizzes and
assignments. It needs this to draw its simplified view.

## Where that data goes

Nowhere. The extension:

- sends requests only to `lms.cuchd.in`, using the session you are already
  logged in with, and only to read pages;
- never sees or stores your password, and cannot read your session cookie;
- has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no remote code;
- never sells, shares or transfers your data to anyone.

To open courses quickly, it keeps a cache of course structures and quiz and
assignment statuses in your browser's `localStorage` for `lms.cuchd.in`. That
cache stays on your device. Clearing the site's data for `lms.cuchd.in`, or
removing the extension, deletes it.

Files you download, including the `.zip` of a course, are built in your
browser from the LMS and saved straight to your computer.
